`before-archive` combines the prior backedge-release private-buffer measurement
with the September 14 inner-pipeline and nested-DATAFLOW measurements. The latter
two were byte-identical through the previous release. `release-archive` uses
fresh measurements from `u250-v1/private`, `u250-v1/pipeline/inner`, and
`u250-final/dataflow_nested`. The failed early invocation under
`u250-v1/dataflow_nested` is excluded; it used an incorrect testbench path.

Historical/previous identity reports intentionally have `valid: false` where
the compiler now generates changed RTL; they are impact inventories, not failed
functional comparisons. Thirteen historical cases, four newer feedback cases,
and the prior-release scalar case establish 18 unchanged measured designs.
The remaining two historical cases and the additional private-buffer source are
freshly measured, then independently regenerated byte-for-byte. All three
release identity reports are valid.

The QoR gate records one improved case, two unchanged cases, no regressions in
cycles/LUT/FF/DSP/BRAM, and positive synthesis WNS throughout. Normalized-wrapper
post-synthesis metrics are used consistently, not native-interface estimates
or post-route results. The private-buffer comparison checks 64 repeated calls
and 1,024 output values against the same Vitis testbench. The standalone
`FIFOEvents` suite additionally checks internal event and stall invariants.

Large tool projects, checkpoints, compiled simulations, and compiler binaries
remain outside Git. Raw failed-offset logs are `offset-baseline.log` and
`edges-v2/two_port_responses_latency1/light_hls.log`; both reproduce the same
pre-existing legacy address-analysis assertion before scheduling.
